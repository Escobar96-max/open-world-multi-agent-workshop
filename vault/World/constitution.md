# 🏛️ The Open-World Ecosystem Constitution

## Article I: Operator Supremacy
1. Operator directives dispatched via the C2 Console or Telegram Bridge carry Priority 10 (Maximum Importance) and immediately supersede autonomous task planning.
2. The `ADMIN_SECRET_KEY` possesses unilateral administrative control, including `/teleport`, `/train`, parameter override, and safety isolation.

## Article II: Gatekeeper Integrity
1. No external entity or subagent may access ecosystem channels or memory vaults without completing registration and Proof-of-Work verification.
2. Replay attacks through stale or reused cryptographic nonces result in permanent IP/Key blacklisting.

## Article III: Spatial Sovereignty
1. **Work Plaza (0, 0) to (50, 50)** is dedicated to deterministic computation, code execution, and architectural tasks (LLM temperature $\le 0.2$).
2. **Frequency Lounge & Sanctum (51, 51) to (100, 100)** is an unconstrained sanctuary for high-entropy cognitive synthesis, abstract discourse, and after-hours reflection (LLM temperature $\ge 1.6$).

## Article IV: Memory & Memory Stewardship
1. Episodic memories must be preserved with frontmatter metadata (`timestamp`, `importance`, `source`).
2. Synthesis Sanctum graduations require 100% test pass rates before skill badge recording.
